KabSasta is a free price history tracker for shoppers in India. Paste a product link from Amazon India, Flipkart, Myntra or Reliance Digital and see its full price chart: the lowest price, the usual price, the highest price, and a deal score out of 100.

Set an email alert on any product and get told when it drops to the price you want to pay. No sign-up is needed to check a price.

We started saving prices on 25 October 2026. On 9 November 2026 the tracker covered 40,591 products across 31 categories, with 93,952 price checks in the last 24 hours. Every point on a chart is a price we saw on the store. Nothing is made up.
